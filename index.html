Filling out the report was never the hard part.

Most small shops already have a form. It sits on a clipboard in the truck or in a folder at the office. What goes missing is everything after it: who fixes the problem, by when, and whether anyone checks the fix.

TeamWorkt keeps the report and the fix on one record, so the follow-up doesn't depend on somebody remembering. We started it to take that paperwork off the clipboard for mom-and-pop companies that don't want to pay for a big safety system.

One report, start to finish

Every report becomes a single record. Each person who works on it adds to the same history, and it doesn't close until a manager has looked at the fix.

Your crew sends it in. A manager reviews it and hands the fix to someone with a due date. That person does the work and adds a photo, and the manager checks it before closing it out.

A sample record. The details are made up, but the status names are the ones you'd see in the app.

What you see

One screen for everything that's open. You can see who owns each fix, what's running late and what's waiting on you, from a phone, a tablet or the office computer.

TeamWorkt Safety

Nothing to download.

TeamWorkt runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Anyone who wants it on their home screen can add it there, and it opens like any other app.

If you can get into your company's domain settings, we can put it at your own address, like safety.yourcompany.com. Skipping separate iPhone and Android apps also keeps the cost down.

Getting set up

You don't configure anything. You send us a few things, and we build TeamWorkt around how you already work.

You send us

  • Your logo and colors
  • Who will use it, and who handles safety
  • The kinds of reports you want from your crew

We handle

  • The report form and what's on it
  • Who can see what, and who gets notified
  • How fixes get assigned and checked
  • A walkthrough with whoever runs safety at your company

Then you get a link and a QR code for a sign like the one at the top of this page. If your process has a step we didn't think of, tell us.

Security, in plain terms

  • Each customer's deployment is designed to run on its own, with its own database, instead of sharing one with every other company.
  • Who can see and change reports is set by role, and it's checked against a go-live checklist before any real data goes in.
  • Everything between your crew's phones and TeamWorkt travels over HTTPS.

What we don't claim

TeamWorkt Safety is not SOC 2 audited or ISO/IEC 27001 certified. We won't say it is until independent auditors have actually done that work.
