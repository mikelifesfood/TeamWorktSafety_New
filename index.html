A sample form. Yours probably looks a lot like it.

You probably already have a form like this.

It lives on a clipboard in the truck, or in a folder back at the office. Filling it out is the easy part.

The hard part is everything after. Who fixes the problem, by when, and does anyone check?

TeamWorkt keeps the report and the fix on the same record, so follow-up doesn't depend on somebody's memory. We built it for mom-and-pop shops that want their safety records off paper without paying for a big safety system.

What your crew sees

A short form that opens from a link or a QR code posted on the job. They say what happened and where, add a photo if they have one, and send it. Most reports take about a minute.

  • English or SpanishOne tap switches the whole form, so nobody has to translate for a coworker.
  • Their name is optionalPeople can report without signing it. If they leave a phone number or email, you can follow up or thank them.
  • Emergencies come firstThe form tells people to call 911 and their supervisor for anything happening right now. It's for reporting, not for getting help on the spot.

What happens after someone hits send

Every report becomes one record. Each person who touches it adds to the same page, and it doesn't close until a manager has looked at the fix.

What you see

One screen with every open report, who owns each fix, and what's running late. New and overdue items sit at the top under Needs your attention.

When a fix is done, the person who did it adds a note and a photo. You look it over and either close it or send it back.

Closed reports keep their whole history: the first report, the notes, the photos and who signed off.

No app store and nothing to install.

TeamWorkt runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Your crew opens a link and they're in. If someone wants it on their home screen, it can sit there like any other app.

If you can get into your company's domain settings, we can put it at your own web address, so your crew sees your name instead of ours. Skipping separate iPhone and Android apps also keeps the cost down for a smaller company.

Getting set up

You don't have to configure software. You send us a few things, and we build it around the way your company already works.

What we need from you

  • Your logo and colors
  • The people who'll use it, and who handles safety
  • The kinds of reports you want your crew sending

What we do

We set up the report form, everyone's role, how fixes get assigned, and who gets notified. Then we walk through it with whoever runs safety at your company and change what needs changing. When it's right, you get a link and a QR code to hand to the crew.
