Our deployment model

Designed to reduce unnecessary shared risk.

TeamWorkt Safety is built for smaller employers that want a focused safety workflow without putting every customer into one large shared application environment. Each production customer deployment is designed to have its own application environment, data project, authentication configuration, storage policies, deployment settings, and secrets.

Security controls are verified during customer implementation.

Before a production customer goes live, the deployment is reviewed against a defined hardening checklist covering authorization, storage access, secrets, encrypted connections, backups, logging, account lifecycle, and export/deletion procedures.

Core security approach

The controls we build around each customer deployment.

01

Dedicated environment

Customer deployments are designed to use separate application and data environments, helping reduce cross-customer exposure and simplifying customer-specific access and offboarding.

02

Least-privilege access

Production hardening includes role-based application access, database row-level authorization, private storage where appropriate, and explicit policies for who can read or change records.

03

Encrypted connections

Customer-facing traffic is served over HTTPS/TLS. The planned Supabase platform automatically enforces SSL for its HTTP APIs, with database SSL enforcement available as an additional production control.

04

Administrative protection

Our security standard requires protected infrastructure accounts, unique credentials, multi-factor authentication for administrative services, and server-side handling of privileged secrets.

05

Backups & recovery

Customer production setup includes a defined backup and recovery approach appropriate to the deployment, plus restoration testing before recovery promises are made contractually.

06

Account lifecycle

Customer onboarding includes defined invitation, role-change, suspension, offboarding, and administrator-recovery procedures so access can be removed when a person's responsibilities change.

07

Data lifecycle

Retention, customer export, deletion, legal holds, and attachment handling are defined by record type, applicable requirements, and the customer agreement rather than one blanket retention period.

08

Security review

Before customer launch, TeamWorkt reviews authorization behavior, storage permissions, exposed secrets, authentication, logging, backup readiness, and common abuse cases.

09

Customer identity options

Where appropriate, customer deployments can be configured around the customer's identity environment, including supported Microsoft, Google, OAuth/OIDC, or SAML-based sign-in patterns.

Security & compliance roadmap

Building the program before claiming the badge.

Our cybersecurity program is being organized around the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover. We are also developing our information security program toward alignment with ISO/IEC 27001, with formal certification included in the longer-term assurance roadmap as the business grows.

We will not describe TeamWorkt Safety as SOC 2 compliant, SOC 2 certified, ISO/IEC 27001 certified, or HIPAA compliant unless and until the applicable work and independent assurance actually support that statement.

About our cloud platform

Our planned customer data platform is Supabase. Supabase states that its hosted platform is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant and ISO 27001 certified.

Those provider controls do not automatically make TeamWorkt Safety compliant or certified. Application configuration, access policies, data handling, customer requirements, and TeamWorkt's own processes remain our responsibility.
